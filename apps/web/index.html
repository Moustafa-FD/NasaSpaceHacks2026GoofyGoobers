How the measurements are compared
  1. Decode: NASA GIBS tiles are colour-coded. Pixel values are colormap-class midpoints of the rendered product, not Level-2 retrievals with quality flags.
  2. Compare: A 3×3-pixel window over the site is compared with a surrounding ring. Nearby changes do not establish facility causation.
  3. Remove seasons: Each calendar month's long-run mean is subtracted, then averaged by year.
  4. Trend: Mann-Kendall tests consistency; Sen's slope estimates the rate per decade.
  5. Before / after: Welch t-test on monthly anomalies, adjusted for autocorrelation, with a 95% confidence interval.

p < 0.05 is marked significant. A non-significant result does not prove there is no effect. Source pixels can be larger than individual buildings.
